The Mantle of the True Phantom

Legendary · Requires Attunement

Armor (Studded Leather)

+3 Armor Class
3 Flicker Charges
30 ft. Teleport Range
4d6 Bonus Psychic Damage

Description

Woven from enchanted shadow and stitched with threads stolen from the Ethereal Plane, this extraordinary armor is more than protection. It is a weapon of deception, a veil against the world, and a silent companion to those who walk between identities.

While wearing this armor, you gain a +3 bonus to AC. As a bonus action, you can speak its command word and transform its appearance into clothing or another kind of armor. You choose its colors, style, and accessories, and it retains its light weight.

The illusion is tactile and flawless. Physical contact reveals nothing unusual, and your equipment, including weapons, can be hidden or seamlessly blended into your disguise. The illusion lasts until you change it with another bonus action or remove the armor.

Legendary Properties

Passive Ability

✧ Perfect Mimicry

You have advantage on Charisma (Deception) and Charisma (Performance) checks made to maintain a disguise or impersonate a specific individual while using the armor's illusion.

Passive Ability

✧ Weightless Grace

The armor makes no sound, regardless of your movements. You have advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks, and you cannot be tracked by non-magical means.

Reaction · 3 Charges

✧ Flicker Shift

When a creature hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to expend one charge and briefly turn into a translucent shadow.

Roll a d6 and reduce the incoming damage by the result multiplied by 5. You can then immediately teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see.

Recharge: The armor regains all expended charges daily at dusk.

Ambush · Psychic Damage

✧ Unseen Malice

If you attack a creature while disguised as someone they consider a trusted ally or a harmless bystander, you gain advantage on your first attack roll against them.

If the attack hits, it deals an additional 4d6 psychic damage as the psychological shock of the betrayal breaks their resolve.
